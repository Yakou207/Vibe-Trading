# Scoring v2: rank-based composite multi-factor score (2026-10-20)

A research track. It does not change the counted strategy's score (`features/factors.py`, `research/scoring.py`), the frozen policy, the paper account or any evidence file, and it does not drive any target.

## Summary

- One predeclared variant was evaluated on the hold-out once. Its hold-out Rank IC is 0.030 at
  h=5 (t 2.10) and 0.032 at h=20 (t 1.05), roughly half the in-sample training value. Removing the
  2024-09-15..2024-10-31 rally barely changes it (0.034 / 0.039).
- The ordering does not yet pay in the quintiles. The hold-out Pearson IC is about zero and Q5-Q1
  is -0.7% (h=5) and -11.4% (h=20) annualized, with negative monotonicity. Most of that comes from
  the rally window: without it Q5-Q1 is +1.0% / -2.5%. A few large winners in the low-score
  quintile outweigh a correct ordering of the typical stock, the same pattern the factor lab found.
- Value and low risk carry the hold-out (category Rank IC 0.028 and 0.031, t about 2.4).
  Liquidity is weaker (0.017). Momentum 12-1 fades to about zero. Quality/growth reverses
  (-0.022, t -2.27): training gave ROE and revenue growth negative signs, the predeclared rule
  kept them, and in the hold-out higher ROE and growth did better.
- The counted 35/25/25/15 total has almost no ranking power on the same member-days: Rank IC
  0.003 in training and 0.009 in the hold-out at h=5. Its sentiment component is constant, its
  capital-flow component takes 3 values, it reaches at most 82.5 of 100, and 20.9% of member-days
  sit below the 0.8 confidence gate. Across all history, the gate blocks only 3.1% of the 2.85% of
  member-days that reach 70 points.
- v2 is a better ranker than the counted score, but this is not a tradable result: there is no
  costed backtest, and the quintile spread is not positive in the hold-out. It stays a research
  score in the picker. A future change to the counted strategy would need a costed test and a new
  predeclared evidence epoch.

## Predeclared design

The design is fixed in the docstring of `src/fintech_quant/research/scoring_v2.py`, committed before the first hold-out run. In short:

- Inputs: the factor lab's neutralized factor z-scores (MAD winsorize, z-score, OLS residual on Shenwan level-1 dummies plus log market cap, z-score again).
- Categories: value: `ep`, `bp`; quality/growth: `roe`, `growth`; low risk: `vol_60d`, `max_ret_20d`; liquidity: `turnover_20d`; reversal: `rev_5d`; momentum 12-1: `mom_12m_skip1m`.
- Each factor's sign is the sign of its training Rank ICIR at h=5; a category is the equal-weight mean of its sign-aligned factors (a missing factor counts as 0), scaled to unit cross-sectional standard deviation.
- Category weights are proportional to each category's training Rank ICIR at h=5, clipped at 0 and normalized.
- Missing inputs are a neutral 0 and lower a coverage score (available factors / 9); they never cost points. A stock with coverage 0 is not scored.
- The final score is the cross-sectional percentile of the weighted composite, in (0, 100].
- Variants evaluated on the hold-out: 1. No parameter was changed after the hold-out was seen.

## Reading the results

- These are gross, equal-weight, daily-data statistics on 300 large caps; they do not include costs, limit-up/down fills or capacity and are not evidence of tradable profit.
- Training numbers are in-sample by construction. The hold-out is the only out-of-sample evidence, and it was evaluated once.
- The nine factors were specified after the factor-lab report, which displayed hold-out statistics for all 27 factors, so the factor list is not fully blind to the hold-out even though signs and weights are fit on training dates only.
- The v2 score is shown in the picker as a research score. It does not drive the counted strategy, which stays frozen until its evidence window completes.
